# Safety Information

Automotive electrical systems can be damaged by incorrect connections, short circuits, reversed polarity, or applying voltage to the wrong diagnostic terminal.

## Before Use

- Verify the exact vehicle, chassis, model year, and diagnostic connector.
- Confirm the correct diagnostic pin before making any connection.
- Confirm ground and power connections independently.
- Inspect all wiring for exposed conductors and shorts.
- Use insulated test leads.
- Use appropriate circuit protection.
- Avoid probing unknown pins with power.
- Disconnect the reader immediately if wiring becomes hot or behaves unexpectedly.

## Important

This project is intended for users who understand basic automotive electrical safety.

The repository does not provide a universal diagnostic pinout because Mercedes-Benz diagnostic connector assignments can vary by vehicle configuration.

Use reliable service information for the exact vehicle being tested.
